## PRACTICAL TIPS  

| Topic | Advice |
|-------|--------|
| **Visa / Entry** | For Bangladeshi citizens traveling domestically, no visa needed. |
| **Weather** | Oct‑Mar is dry, 25‑30 °C. Pack light cotton, a light sweater for evenings, and a rain‑coat if traveling in April‑May. |
| **Health** | Bring basic meds (pain reliever, antihistamine, motion‑sickness tablets). Drink bottled water only. |
| **Currency** | ATMs are common in Dhaka and Cox’s Bazar; carry some cash for small vendors. |
| **Connectivity** | Local SIM (Banglalink, Grameenphone) offers 4G; buy at the airport for ~BDT 150. |
| **Safety** | Cox’s Bazar is tourist‑friendly. Keep valuables in the hotel safe, avoid isolated beaches after dark. |
| **Cultural etiquette** | Dress modestly when away from the beach (shoulders covered, knees down). |
