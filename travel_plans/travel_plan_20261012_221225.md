## 📋 Practical Tips for a Smooth 2‑Day USA Trip  

1. **Visa** – Ensure you have a valid **U.S. B‑1/B‑2 tourist visa** (or ESTA if you hold dual citizenship with an eligible country). Processing can take 2‑4 weeks.  
2. **Travel Insurance** – Recommended coverage: $100k medical, trip cancellation, and baggage protection.  
3. **Currency** – USD is cash‑less in most places; a travel card (e.g., Revolut, N26, or a Visa/Mastercard from Bangladeshi banks) works everywhere. Carry a small amount of cash for tips.  
4. **Time‑zone adjustment** – NYC is **10 hours behind** Bangladesh. Try to sleep on the flight to reduce jet‑lag.  
5. **Packing** – Early October NYC weather: **10‑18 °C (50‑65 °F)**. Pack a light jacket, comfortable walking shoes, and a small umbrella.  
6. **Connectivity** – Buy a prepaid **US SIM** (T‑Mobile, AT&T) at the airport; $30 for 2 GB data + unlimited texting.  
7. **Safety** – NYC is safe, but keep valuables close in crowded places (subway, Times Square).  
8. **Tipping** – Standard US tipping: 15‑20 % for restaurants, $1‑2 per bag for hotel bellhop, $2‑5 for taxi rides.
